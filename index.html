Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Smart College Event Management System?

A centralized platform for planning college events, student registration, notifications, participant tracking and digital certificates.

Who can use this platform?

College administrators, faculty and event coordinators, and students.

How can students register for events?

Students browse upcoming events and register online.

Can administrators manage participants?

Yes, participant tracking is part of the administrator dashboard.

Does the system support digital certificates?

Yes, digital certificate generation is included.

Can the platform be customized?

Yes, customization depends on the agreed scope.

What is the annual pricing?
